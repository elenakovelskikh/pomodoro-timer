Privacy Policy

Violet Tomato Discord Activity · Last updated: November 18, 2026

The Activity is a shared Pomodoro timer for Discord calls and channels. It doesn't ask for access to your Discord account and doesn't receive your name, avatar, messages, voice or video. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

What is stored

So that everyone in the Activity sees the same timer, its state is kept in Google Firebase Realtime Database (Google LLC, USA; data stored in the EU, region europe-west1): the session settings, the current round or break and when it started, the log of completed rounds and breaks, and which popups are open. It is saved under a random ID that Discord gives each launch of the Activity, is not linked to any person and doesn't record who pressed what.

The state is deleted automatically when the last participant leaves. A session left behind in rare cases is removed by hand.

What is processed when you open it

Discord runs the Activity and passes its traffic through its own proxy, so the services below don't see your IP address. The Activity's files are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which may keep standard request logs. The database library may keep a small technical value in your browser's storage to reconnect faster.

This serves to provide the shared timer (legitimate interest). Google and GitHub are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and keep their data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or transfer of your data, object to its processing, and complain to a data protection authority. As stored sessions aren't linked to anyone, a request needs to say roughly when and in which server or call the session took place.
