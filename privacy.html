Privacy Policy

Violet Tomato, a Pomodoro timer website · Last updated: November 11, 2026

The timer

The timer runs entirely in your browser. Your settings and session log are not stored on your device and are not sent anywhere. There are no accounts and no cookies.

What is processed when you visit

  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver the pages, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request data (date and time, requested page, browser) and may keep them in its logs for security and operation.
  • Visit statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) counts visits. It receives the visited page, the referring page, browser and device type, and an approximate country derived from your IP address. It stores nothing on your device (no cookies, no local storage) and the operator only sees aggregated numbers.

Purpose: delivering and securing the website, and understanding how much it is used. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You are not required to provide any data; there is no profiling or automated decision-making.

Transfer to the USA and retention

GitHub and Cloudflare process data in the USA. Both are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). The operator does not store any of this data. GitHub and Cloudflare keep it as described in their policies (GitHub, Cloudflare).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR). To use them, write to the contact address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live (Art. 77 GDPR).
