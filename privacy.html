Privacy Policy

Violet Tomato, a Pomodoro timer website · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Violet Tomato ("the website", elenakovelskikh.github.io/pomodoro-timer) is a free Pomodoro timer. It is made and operated by Elena Kovelskikh, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the website handles and what it does not. The Violet Tomato Activity for Discord has its own privacy policy.

The short version

There are no accounts, no advertising and no cookies. The timer runs entirely in your browser: your settings, rounds and session log are never sent anywhere and are gone when you close or reload the page. The website counts visits anonymously, without cookies and without storing anything on your device.

The timer

Everything the timer does happens in your browser. The number of rounds, the long break setting, the countdown and the session log exist only while the page is open. Nothing is saved on your device (no cookies, no local storage) and nothing is sent to a server.

Visit statistics

To see how many people use the website, it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. When the page loads, a small script tells Cloudflare that the page was visited. Cloudflare uses the technical information that comes with such a request, such as the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device type, and an approximate country derived from your IP address, to show the operator aggregated counts (for example, visits per day or per country).

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage, does not create a profile of you and does not follow you across other websites. The operator sees only the aggregated numbers, never individual visitors. Because nothing is stored on your device and nothing is used to identify you, no consent banner is shown.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard technical data when your browser requests the website's files, including your IP address, and may keep it in its logs for security and operation, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Service providers

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Web Analytics) counts visits as described above, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Neither receives any information about how you use the timer itself.

Your rights

Under data protection law (such as the GDPR) you can ask what information about you is processed, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. The website itself keeps no personal information about visitors; for the technical data described above, you can also contact GitHub and Cloudflare directly.

Changes to this policy

If the website starts handling other information (for example, saving your settings between visits), this policy will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions and requests can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/elenakovelskikh/pomodoro-timer/issues.
