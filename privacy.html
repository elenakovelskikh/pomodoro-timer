Privacy Policy

Violet Tomato · Last updated: November 11, 2026

The timer runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you do in it is stored or sent anywhere. There are no accounts and no cookies.

What is processed when you visit

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which receives your IP address and standard request data to deliver the pages and may keep them in its logs. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which receives the visited page, the referring page, browser and device type, and an approximate country, and shows only aggregated numbers. It stores nothing on your device.

This serves to run the website and to see how much it is used (legitimate interest). Both providers process data in the USA and are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The operator stores none of this data; the providers keep it under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or transfer of your data, object to its processing, and complain to a data protection authority. To keep your visits out of the statistics, you can block static.cloudflareinsights.com in your browser or ad blocker.
